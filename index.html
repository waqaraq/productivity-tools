Reusable MCQ Quiz Builder

A Google Forms Editor add-on for educators who create weighted multiple-choice assessments and maintain structured Outcome-Based Education (OBE) assessment records.

Google FormsWeighted MCQsOBE ReportingResults WorkbookPDF Evidence

What it does

Build quizzes from Excel

Import MCQ question statements and Options A–D from a simple Excel template and build the active Google Form.

Weighted assessment

Apply configurable A/B/C/D weights and maintain consistent Level 4–1 rubric interpretation across questions.

Automatic results

Calculate student results and maintain a structured Google Sheets Results workbook as Form responses are submitted.

OBE evidence

Generate a complete OBE PDF report with institutional metadata, student performance, question analysis, charts and detailed evidence.

Assessment metadata

The add-on supports University, Department, Course Name, Course Code, Teacher, CLO, mapped PLO, Bloom taxonomy, Problem Statement, rubric levels, scoring weights and quiz-delivery settings.

Student identification

Generated Forms can collect Student Name and Student Registration Number, together with email address when the teacher enables Google Forms email collection.

Privacy by design: assessment responses remain in the teacher's Google Form and Results workbook. The application does not operate a separate developer-controlled database containing copies of student response records.
